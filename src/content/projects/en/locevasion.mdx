## Moving beyond the limits of a static website

Loc'Evasion's previous website presented the company and its vehicles, but customers could not book online. To check availability and complete a rental, they had to call the agency during opening hours.

The new platform removes this constraint by providing a booking journey that remains accessible when the company is closed, preventing enquiries made outside opening hours from being lost.

## A fully automated booking journey

Customers can search for vehicles available over a selected period, choose a model and its options, enter their details, and complete their payment online.

The entire journey is automated through to booking confirmation. No manual approval from the agency is required.

## A direct connection to the business management system

The website does not maintain a separate catalogue. Vehicles, availability, rates and bookings remain fully managed through the company's business management system.

The platform queries this data to show customers real availability. An empty result is treated as no availability, while technical failures remain clearly distinct so the website never displays vehicles that are not actually available.

## My role in the project

The project is being developed by a two-person team. My scope covers the vehicle rental side:

- technical architecture and infrastructure;
- APIs and integration with the business management system;
- the search and booking journey;
- online payment;
- reliable communication between the different services.

My teammate is developing the private transport side of the platform.

## Project status

The booking journey is functional. The project is currently being integrated, with only the final configuration of its different services remaining before it can go live.
